A Tale of Two Worlds

Founded by Chef Arjun Patel, previously of Noma, Éclat Bistro bridges the vibrant, rustic flavors of Odisha's riverside bounty with the exacting precision of Parisian haute cuisine.

We believe in sustainability, sourcing 80% of our ingredients from local farms within a 50km radius, ensuring zero waste while delivering uncompromised luxury.
